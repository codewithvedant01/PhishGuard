    Detailed Inspection Findings:

      ⏰

      Artificial Urgency

      Scammers create immediate panic ("Service disconnected in 2 hours", "Account permanently blocked"). They want you to react out of fear before you have time to think or consult someone.

      🎁

      Too Good to Be True Offers

      Free laptops, government solar schemes, KBC lotteries of ₹25 Lakhs, or earning ₹5,000/day for 15 minutes of work. If you did not enter a contest or apply for a job, you cannot win.

      🔑

      Asking for OTP or UPI PIN

      The Golden Rule: You enter your UPI PIN ONLY to send money, never to receive money. Anyone asking you to enter PIN to receive cashback is scamming you.

      🌐

      Weird Domain Names (.xyz, .top)

      Look closely at website addresses. Official government portals always end in .gov.in. Official banks have proprietary TLDs like .sbi or exact official domains.

      📲

      Screen Sharing Apps (AnyDesk/TeamViewer)

      Scammers ask victims to install apps like RustDesk, AnyDesk, or QuickSupport to "fix their account". This lets them view your screen and steal OTPs and bank credentials in real-time.

      👥

      Impersonation of Relatives

      Receiving messages from a friend's profile asking for immediate money due to an emergency or accident. Always make a direct phone call to verify before sending any money.

      National Cybercrime Reporting Helpline
      Dial 1930

      Toll-free 24x7 helpline operated by the Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA), Government of India.

      Visit cybercrime.gov.in →

      Action Steps in the "Golden Hour" (First 2 Hours After Fraud):

      1

      Call 1930 Immediately

      The Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C) coordinates directly with banks. Calling within the first hour gives the highest chance of freezing the fraudster's account before funds are withdrawn.

      2

      Freeze Cards & UPI in Your Bank App

      Immediately open your mobile banking app to temporarily lock your debit/credit card and block UPI services, or call your bank's official customer care number to freeze the account.

      3

      Preserve All Digital Evidence

      Take screenshots of chat history, call logs, SMS messages, and bank transaction reference numbers (UTR numbers). Do not delete chat logs as they are required for filing an FIR.
